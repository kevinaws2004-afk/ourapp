## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an energetic yet soothing consumer productivity experience designed to counter operational burnout. It merges the warmth of high-end confectionery design with disciplined, precision utility. Moving away from generic bubblegum pinks, the identity centers around an invigorating, sun-infused **Electric Papaya Pastel** harmonized with cool **Aqua Mint** and soothing **Periwinkle Lilac**. 

The emotional tone balances whimsical optimism with uncompromising functional clarity. Every interaction feels tactile, creamy, and gently buoyant—reminiscent of bespoke stationery, lacquerware desk accessories, and modern candy packaging.

### Core Visual Pillars
- **Porcelain Canvas:** A warm, uncompressed off-white foundation (`#FBF9F7`) eliminating sterile monitor glare.
- **Pill & Organic Soft Geometry:** Generous corner radii, floating pill actions, and deeply sculpted cards that feel approachable and touch-friendly.
- **Luminescent Ambient Glow:** Multi-tonal, tinted chromatic drop-shadows replacing dirty monochromatic black drops.
- **Confectionary Color Palette:** Playful, high-chroma pastel accents curated to organize dense lifestyle, task, and wellness data without cognitive strain.

## Colors

The palette avoids the saturated fatigue of neon interfaces while steering clear of muted, washed-out chalky pastels. It leverages saturated pastel mid-tones that hold WCAG AAA compliance when set against deep slate typography.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Electric Papaya / Sunset Coral - `#FF6B4A`):** The signature brand anchor. Used for high-impact CTAs, focal metrics, active states, and delight moments.
- **Secondary (Aqua Mint - `#2DD4BF`):** Refreshing balance to coral warmth. Drives completion states, streaks, financial or wellness logs, and positive momentum indicators.
- **Tertiary (Periwinkle Lilac - `#818CF8`):** Deep cognitive calm. Used for scheduling, deep-work categorization, reflection journals, and secondary badges.
- **Neutral (Midnight Slate - `#1E232F`):** Anchors readability with ultra-crisp, high-contrast text and micro-borders, preventing the pastel environment from feeling juvenile.
- **Canvas & Surface:**
  - Background: Warm Porcelain (`#FBF9F7`)
  - Elevated Cards: Pure Cloud White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Subtle Wells & Inputs: Soft Cream Silk (`#F4EFEA`)
- **Functional Pastel Spectrum (Categories & Tags):**
  - Butter Peach: `#FDBA74`
  - Sky Breeze: `#38BDF8`
  - Pistachio Cream: `#86EFAC`
  - Soft Lemon Custard: `#FDE047`

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric build, open apertures, and distinctive humanist curvature echo the pill-shaped, confectionery character of the interface without devolving into display novelty.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Rendered in ExtraBold (800) and Bold (700) with slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to create snappy, confident brand banners and dashboard summaries.
- **Body Text:** Uses Medium (500) and Regular (400) weights set in high-contrast Midnight Slate (`#1E232F`). Muted notes and subtitles shift to Slate Muted (`#64748B`), never dipping below 4.5:1 contrast against `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F7`.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Pill indicators, chip counters, and tab bars leverage SemiBold (600) and Bold (700) uppercase tracking to retain crisp legibility against pastel pill fills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a comfortable, breathable **fluid grid** calibrated around 4px and 8px rhythmic steps. Screens prioritize airy breathing room over hyper-dense data compression, framing each widget as a clean confection in a gift box.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Cards span 4 columns with stacked metadata. Bottom-anchored pill navigation remains fixed with safe-area padding.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px). Supports dual-column dashboard structures.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1440px` with `margin-lg: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter-lg: 1.75rem` (28px). Supports flexible 3-to-4 card modular widgets.

### Spacing Dynamics
Component interiors consistently use generous inner breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl` for cards; `space-sm` to `space-md` for buttons and interactive controls). Stacked elements rely on `space-md` (16px) to maintain grouping relationships without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely rejects sharp, opaque, cold gray drop-shadows. Elevation is achieved through a hybrid of **chromatic ambient glow**, **tonal stacking**, and **subtle boundary rim lighting**.

### Elevation Scale
1. **Flat (Canvas Tier):** Grounded `#FBF9F7` surface. Zero shadow. Used as the main backdrop.
2. **Surface Sunken (Level 0):** Inset controls such as form inputs and inactive track wells use `#F4EFEA` with a soft inset perimeter: `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px rgba(30, 35, 47, 0.03)`.
3. **Elevated Card (Level 1):** Primary white `#FFFFFF` cards resting on the porcelain canvas. Retains a compound shadow system:
   - Ambient drop: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.06)` (a faint Papaya whisper).
   - Base drop: `0 4px 12px 0 rgba(30, 35, 47, 0.03)`.
   - Structural rim: A delicate border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` layered over `1px solid rgba(30, 35, 47, 0.04)`.
4. **Floating Action & Popover (Level 2):** Dropdown menus, modal sheets, and active quick-action buttons.
   - Ambient glow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.14)` for primary actions, or `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.16)` for mint-accented modules.
5. **Interactive Press:** On click or tap, controls depress by `-2px` transform with an adjusted, tighter colored halo (`0 4px 12px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.2)`), offering an elastic, satisfying spring.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** roundedness, prioritizing fluid geometric comfort.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Buttons, Badges, Tabs, Pills:** Fully circular radii (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards, Panels, and Sheets:** Extra-deep rounded surfaces using `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px depending on display viewport), evoking rounded ceramic trays.
- **Nested Inner Items (Avatars, Mini Badges, Icon Wells):** 16px to 20px (`rounded-2xl`) to establish concentric harmony within the master card frame.
- **Form Inputs:** Fully rounded pill envelopes (9999px) for single-line inputs and search fields; generous `24px` radius for multi-line textareas.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Hero Button:** Pure Electric Papaya (`#FF6B4A`) background, high-contrast white (`#FFFFFF`) bold text, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Elevated by a matching papaya glow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.35)`. Hover yields a gentle scale transform (`scale(1.02)`) and intensified warmth.
- **Secondary Mint Action:** Aqua Mint (`#2DD4BF`) background with Midnight Slate (`#1E232F`) text. Elevated with a mint ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.3)`.
- **Soft Confection Button (Subtle):** Tinted pastel background (e.g., `#FFF0EB` for Papaya soft; `#E6FAF7` for Mint soft) with matching full-saturation foreground text and zero baseline shadow.

### Cards & Modules
- **Main Widget Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed in a 28px (`rounded-3xl`) radius. Padding is set to a generous `24px` or `32px`. Each card features a discreet header zone, high-contrast `#1E232F` typography, and an optional 4px accent line or tinted pastel icon halo.
- **Tinted Category Variant Cards:** Soft full-surface tinted cards (e.g., Butter Peach at 15% opacity over white) paired with full-chroma status pills for grouping calendars, habits, and projects.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full`) with `padding: 4px 14px`. Utilizes pastel fills paired with high-contrast text:
  - Active / Urgent: Sunset Coral (`#FF6B4A`) text on `#FFF0EC`.
  - Done / Healthy: Aqua Mint (`#0F766E`) text on `#E6FAF8`.
  - Focus / Routine: Periwinkle (`#4338CA`) text on `#EEF2FF`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pill-shaped frames with a warm porcelain-sand well (`#F4EFEA`), transparent borders, and deep slate text. On focus: morphs to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) backed by a crisp 2px primary Papaya ring (`#FF6B4A`) and a subtle `0 0 0 4px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.15)` focus spread.
- **Checkboxes & Toggle Switches:**
  - Toggles: Pill tracks with a 4px inner inset. Inactive is `#E2DDD8`; active switches immediately to `#2DD4BF` (Mint) with an elevated pure white marble thumb carrying a soft colored drop.
  - Checkboxes: 20px rounded squares (`rounded-lg`) filled with `#2DD4BF` when checked, showing a clean white glyph.

### Lists & Progress Tracks
- **Task List Rows:** Individual floating pills or rounded ribbons separated by `8px` gaps instead of divider lines. Striking or completing tasks activates an Aqua Mint celebration glow with strikethrough slate-muted text.
- **Meter / Progress Bars:** Smooth 12px pill capsules with `#F4EFEA` backgrounds and multi-stage gradient fills transitioning from Sunset Coral (`#FF6B4A`) to Butter Peach (`#FDBA74`) or Aqua Mint (`#2DD4BF`).
