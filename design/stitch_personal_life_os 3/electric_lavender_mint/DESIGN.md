## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, sensory-rich consumer lifestyle aesthetic that marries digital calm with tactile confectionery playfulness. Drawing inspiration from modern tactile minimalism and pastel glassmorphism, it moves away from expected bubblegum tones into an intoxicating, luminous pairing of Electric Lavender and Fresh Mint.

The emotional target is uplifting, joyful clarity: the interface feels like an organized, dreamy sanctuary where productivity and daily habits feel delightfully light.

### Visual Principles
- **Confectionery Tactility:** Surfaces feel smooth, pillowy, and clean—like spun porcelain and glazed pastel surfaces.
- **Luminous Radiance:** Light is treated as a tint rather than a pure drop shadow; soft, atmospheric purple and mint glows delineate elevated interactive surfaces.
- **Pill Fluidity:** Interfaces prioritize soft pill silhouettes, pebble containers, and organic flowing layouts to retain friendly warmth across all touchpoints.
- **Chromatic Play with Grounded Readability:** Tagging and status micro-moments introduce candy-pastel variety (butter peach, sky blue, lilac), anchored firmly by deep indigo-charcoal typography to guarantee effortless contrast.

## Colors

The palette balances ethereal tint washes with punchy, saturated interaction anchors.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Brand (`#7C5CFC` / `#8B5CF6`):** Electric Lavender and Violet Periwinkle. Used for active navigation tabs, major identity touches, selected pill states, and energetic graphic highlights.
- **Secondary Action (`#10B981` / `#2DD4BF` / `#34D399`):** Fresh Mint and Crisp Spearmint. Reserved for primary conversion actions, completed status items, success feedback, toggles, and positive progression.
- **Canvas & Background (`#F8F9FE` / `#F4F5FD`):** Lavender-tinted porcelain. A pale, slightly cool wash that keeps white cards distinctly crisp and floating without visual harshness.
- **Surface Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-opacity pure white used on cards, floating bottom sheets, and modal views.
- **Deep Neutral Typography (`#1E1B4B` / `#1F2438`):** Deep indigo-charcoal used for text and primary icons to ensure accessible legibility against pastel containers. Muted body text shifts to `#6366F1` at 70% opacity or `#64748B`.

### Multi-Colored Pastel Pill Accents
Used for category tagging, badges, and contextual chip grouping:
- **Butter Peach:** Surface `#FFF1E6`, Content `#C2410C`
- **Soft Sky:** Surface `#E0F2FE`, Content `#0369A1`
- **Spearmint Pasture:** Surface `#DCFCE7`, Content `#15803D`
- **Pastel Lilac:** Surface `#EDE9FE`, Content `#6D28D9`

## Typography

The typography is built around Plus Jakarta Sans, chosen for its welcoming geometric warmth, open apertures, and contemporary editorial poise.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Headline Tracking:** Tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) is applied to titles and displays to create solid, friendly headline groupings.
- **Weight Pairing:** Headlines heavily leverage Bold (`700`) and ExtraBold (`800`), contrasting with light, airy canvas space and regular body text.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small chips employ SemiBold and Bold weights with mild positive tracking to retain punchiness at miniature sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured fluid grid optimized for soft spatial grouping. Content lives within generous safe-areas, framed by breathing room that emphasizes the soft porcelain background.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column dynamic flow with `margin: 1.25rem`. Cards stretch edge-to-edge within margins to maximize touch surface.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, `margin-tablet: 2rem`, `gutter-tablet: 1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout centered with max-width `1280px`, `margin-desktop: 3rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2rem`.

### Spacing Usage
- `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-text gaps within micro badges.
- `space-sm` (8px): Spacing inside input fields and between inline pill items.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding and vertical rhythm between text lines.
- `space-lg` (24px): Gap between distinct component cards in a stack.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section narrative separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses colored ambient dispersion rather than desaturated grey shadows, creating an illuminated, confectionery presence.

### Depth System
1. **Flat / Floor Level (Elevation 0):** The base porcelain background (`#F8F9FE`).
2. **Elevated Cards (Elevation 1):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-border of `1px solid rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.08)` and an ambient dual-shadow:
   - Primary softness: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.08)`
   - Immediate contact: `0 2px 6px 0 rgba(31, 36, 56, 0.02)`
3. **Interactive & Floating Modals (Elevation 2):** Pure white or semi-transparent glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`). Surrounded by an expanded aura:
   - Floating glow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.14), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.06)`
4. **Primary Mint Call-to-Action Hover:**
   - Active Mint button glow: `0 10px 20px -5px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The shape system embraces a full pill aesthetic (roundedness scale 3). Edges are radically smooth and organic, avoiding sharp mechanical vertices.

### Corner Archetypes
- **Buttons, Badges, Chips, Text Inputs:** Fully rounded capsules (`9999px` border radius).
- **Cards & Content Blocks:** Generous pebble containers with `rounded-3xl` (`2rem` / `32px` corner radius).
- **Sheet Overlays & Modals:** `rounded-t-[2.5rem]` on mobile viewports for soft sheet pull-ups.
- **Avatar & Icon Surrounds:** Continuous circular profiles or squircle pebble silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Mint Conversion):** Solid `#10B981` background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), fully pill-shaped. Hover state darkens slightly to `#059669` accompanied by a mint ambient shadow.
- **Brand Signature (Electric Lavender):** Solid `#7C5CFC` background, white text, fully pill-shaped. Used for non-conversion high-priority actions like sharing, profile creation, and core feature triggers.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Soft lavender tint background (`#EDE9FE`), text color `#7C5CFC`, no border.

### Chips & Tags
- **Shape & Sizing:** Fully pill-shaped with `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Palette Allocations:**
  - *Peach Chip:* `#FFF1E6` background, `#EA580C` text.
  - *Sky Chip:* `#E0F2FE` background, `#0284C7` text.
  - *Mint Chip:* `#DCFCE7` background, `#16A34A` text.
  - *Lilac Chip:* `#EDE9FE` background, `#7C5CFC` text.
- **Selection State:** White text with filled brand background when active.

### Cards
- **Card Containers:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `border-radius: 2rem`, `padding: 1.5rem`, wrapped in a micro-border `rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive State:** Mild upward translation (`translate-y: -2px`) paired with a boosted electric lavender ambient shadow.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Container:** Fully pill-shaped field with `#F4F5FD` soft fill, `1px solid rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.12)`, deep indigo text (`#1E1B4B`).
- **Focus State:** White background fill, outline replaced by `2px solid #7C5CFC` and a soft focus ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.15)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth rounded-lg (`8px`) box. Unchecked: `2px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked: Vibrant `#10B981` fill with white checkmark.
- **Toggles / Switches:** Full pill track in `#E2E8F0`. Active state switches to `#10B981` with a floating pure white circular knob casting a slight drop shadow.

### Lifestyle Metric / Habit Rings
- Complementary circular progress trackers utilizing `#10B981` for active stroke and `#EDE9FE` for remaining track, accented with soft rounded stroke terminals.
