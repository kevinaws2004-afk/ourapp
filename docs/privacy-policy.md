# DayMeter Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

DayMeter is designed to keep your information private.

## What we collect
Nothing. DayMeter does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

## Where your data lives
Everything you enter in DayMeter — plans, activities, records, measurements, goals, routines, notes, your name and settings — is stored only in a database on your device.

DayMeter also keeps a few automatic backup files of that data in its private storage on your device (the last 7 days). You can turn this off in **Me → Backup & restore**.

## Android device backup
If you have Android's own device backup turned on (in your phone's settings, managed by Google), Android may include DayMeter's database and its backup files in your device backup, so they move to a new phone. This is a feature of Android that you control, encrypted by Google under Google's privacy policy; DayMeter itself never uploads anything.

## Notifications, app lock and Do Not Disturb
Reminders and timer notifications are created on your device. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock; DayMeter never sees or stores your biometric data. If you turn on Do Not Disturb for focus sessions, DayMeter only switches your phone's Do Not Disturb mode on and off.

## Focus-mode app blocking (Accessibility)
If you turn on **Me → Focus → Block distracting apps**, DayMeter asks you to enable its Accessibility service ("DayMeter focus blocking"). It is used only for this feature: while a focus session is running, it notices which app comes to the front, and if it is one you chose to block, it brings DayMeter back to the front.

- It reads only the name of the app that opened. It never reads what is on your screen, what you type, or anything inside other apps.
- It does not record which apps you use; nothing is stored about them except the list of apps you chose to block.
- Nothing is sent anywhere.
- It does nothing when no focus session is running. You can turn it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings or in DayMeter.

## Error log
DayMeter keeps a short log of recent errors on your device (error types and technical details, not what you write in the app). It never leaves your phone.

## Analytics, ads and tracking
DayMeter contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Exporting and deleting
You can export a full backup or spreadsheets from **Me → Backup & restore**; you choose where those files go. You can delete any item in the app, and uninstalling DayMeter removes all of its data from your device.

## Children
DayMeter does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
