# Daylog Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Daylog is designed to keep your information private.

## What we collect
Nothing. Daylog does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

## Where your data lives
Everything you enter in Daylog — plans, activities, records, measurements, goals, routines, notes, your name and settings — is stored only in a database on your device.

Daylog also keeps a few automatic backup files of that data in its private storage on your device (the last 7 days). You can turn this off in **Me → Backup & restore**.

## Android device backup
If you have Android's own device backup turned on (in your phone's settings, managed by Google), Android may include Daylog's database and its backup files in your device backup, so they move to a new phone. This is a feature of Android that you control, encrypted by Google under Google's privacy policy; Daylog itself never uploads anything.

## Health Connect
If you choose **Import** under **Me → Body measurements**, Daylog asks Android's Health Connect for permission to **read** your weight, height and body fat percentage. Daylog uses this only to add those measurements to your body measurements on your device. It reads only when you tap Import, never writes to Health Connect, and never sends this data anywhere. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Health Connect.

## Notifications, app lock and Do Not Disturb
Reminders and timer notifications are created on your device. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock; Daylog never sees or stores your biometric data. If you turn on Do Not Disturb for focus sessions, Daylog only switches your phone's Do Not Disturb mode on and off.

## Problem reports and feedback
Daylog keeps a short log of recent errors on your device (error types and technical details, not what you write in the app) to help fix problems. It is sent only if you choose **Me → Send feedback → Share a problem report** and pick where to send it. Feedback messages are sent with the app you choose.

## Analytics, ads and tracking
Daylog contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Exporting and deleting
You can export a full backup or spreadsheets from **Me → Backup & restore**; you choose where those files go. You can delete any item in the app, and uninstalling Daylog removes all of its data from your device.

## Children
Daylog does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
