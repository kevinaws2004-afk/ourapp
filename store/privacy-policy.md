# Daylog Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Daylog is designed to keep your information private.

## What we collect
Nothing. Daylog does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## Where your data lives
Everything you enter in Daylog — plans, activities, records, measurements, goals, your name and settings — is stored only in a database on your device. There is no account, no server and no cloud sync.

## Analytics, ads and tracking
Daylog contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Exporting and deleting
You can export a full backup from **Me → Export your data**; you choose where that file goes. You can delete any item in the app, and uninstalling Daylog removes all of its data from your device.

## Children
Daylog does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
