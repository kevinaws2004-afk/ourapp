## Full description (4000 chars max)
Daylog is a calm, private planner and tracker for everything you do — gym, reading, deep work, walks, study, meditation, or anything you invent.

PLAN YOUR DAY
• See today at a glance: what's next, what's done, and how much time you've tracked.
• Add things at a time or "anytime", and repeat them on the days you choose.
• Shape the week ahead with a clear week strip and month calendar.

DO IT, AND RECORD IT
• One tap on the + starts any activity right now, with a timer if it needs one.
• Log exactly what matters: sets and reps for the gym, pages for reading, distance for a walk.
• "Use last time" fills in what you did before, so logging takes seconds.

TRACK ANYTHING, YOUR WAY
• Start from ready-made activities, or build your own with the fields you choose: numbers, durations, ratings, checklists, choices, and repeating groups like sets.
• Body measurements: weight, waist, body fat and more, each with its own chart.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Things done, time tracked, active days and plan completion, compared with last period.
• A consistency calendar, where your time went, and plan vs. reality.
• Goals with streaks: "30 days of reading", "21 days of meditation".
• Build your own charts from any activity and field.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No sign-in. No ads. No tracking.
• Everything stays on your phone. Export a full backup whenever you want.

BEAUTIFUL, DAY AND NIGHT
• Three hand-tuned themes — Lavender, Rose and Papaya — each in light and dark.

Daylog: plan it, do it, see it add up.
